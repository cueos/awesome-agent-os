# Personal agents and companion chatbots

## Scope

This list connects products people can use with the components for building
agents. Personal agents carry out tasks and ongoing responsibilities across
tools. Companion chatbots focus on recurring conversation, character, and
companionship. They share questions about memory, voice, and continuity, so
both belong here with their main purposes visible.

This grouping is an editorial choice, rather than a claim that the categories
have separate underlying technology. Compare agents through task
follow-through and control, and companions through conversational continuity
and character. A conversational example alone does not demonstrate an action
in another app.

source checks of advertised capabilities and access terms, not hands-on tests

## Evidence limits

No accounts were created and no tasks, purchases, conversations, or permission
flows were tested for this review. Muse's app redirected to sign-in and its
public product page exposed no readable text to the fetcher; its capability
description therefore relies on Meta's public announcement. Character.AI's
app page also exposed no readable text, so its help documentation supplies the
feature evidence.

[Riley Brown's product comparison](https://x.com/rileybrown/status/2113773696863064377)
and [Julie Zhuo's social AI essay](https://x.com/joulee/status/2110115516555903182)
are useful perspectives on what people want from personal AI. The entries
above use official sources for capability and availability claims.
